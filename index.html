What makes this a free Loom alternative?

Loom-ish is a free, open-source browser screen recorder for people who want screen, microphone and webcam capture without creating an account or uploading video to a hosted library. Your recording is created locally, then you download and save the file yourself.

Free Loom alternative FAQ

Is this screen recorder really free?

Yes. Loom-ish is a free, open-source screen recorder. It does not require an account or a paid plan.

Where are my recordings stored?

They are not stored by this website. Recording happens in your browser, and you must download and save the finished video to your own device.

Does this upload my screen recording?

No. Your screen, webcam and microphone streams are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to this website.

How is this different from Loom?

Loom-ish is designed for private, local recording without an account or hosted video library. Loom is designed around cloud-hosted sharing, collaboration and team video management.

Which browsers work best?

Chrome and Edge provide the best experience, including the floating webcam bubble. Other modern desktop browsers may support recording with fewer controls.

Uses your browser's screen-share and camera permissions directly — this page never sends video anywhere. The floating bubble uses the Document Picture-in-Picture API (Chrome/Edge); other browsers fall back to a plain floating video without the built-in stop button. To prevent duplicate webcam images, full-screen captures use the floating bubble only, while tab and window captures add one circular webcam bubble to the saved video. Trimming and speed changes re-encode the selected range, so boundaries can land roughly a quarter of a second off from the slider. Lower resolution, frame rate, or quality reduces CPU and memory use while recording; the defaults above are already set to a light-power preset.
